# FACTORS INFLUENCING CALORIE BURN AMONG ADULTS
## Problem Statement
 Most adults aspire to loose weight and one question almost everyone asks themselves is; "What can i do to burn more calories?" For some, starting off the fitness journey becomes a challenge
 due to fear arising from demographic , health status and lifestyle differences.  
 This study is focused on exploring how demographic, health condition and other physical activity patterns contribute to number of calories burned.
 The goal is to uncover meaningful patterns that can inform personalized fitness recommandations and improve health outcomes.  

 ## Objectives
   1.  Identify effective physical activities that can be considered for calorie burn
   2.  To determine if other lifestyle behaviors and health conditions  affect number of calories being burned
   3.  Explore gender and age based differences in fitness behaviors.
      
## Scope
 The scope of this project is to analyze patterns, trends, and relationships in health and fitness data to understand the factors that influence calories burned among participants.
Using data collected from activity tracking devices, the analysis focuses on 3000 participants who took part in different physical activities,presented varying health coditions and
lifestyle behaviors from January to December 2024.  
To achieve the project objective project analysis criteria was considered.  
  -  Data cleanup
  -  univariate analysis
  -  bivariate analysis
  -  multivariate analysis
 
 ## 1. Identify effective physical activities that can be considered for calorie burn
 - From the study it was discovered that physical activities may burn different amounts of calories depending on the internsity.  
 - Activities like High intensity interval training (HIIT), running and cycling lead to higher amount of calorie burn.  
 - Walking and yoga led to less calorie burn, they are less intensive activies as well.
