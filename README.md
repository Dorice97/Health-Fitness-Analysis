# FACTORS INFLUENCING CALORIE BURN AMONG ADULTS

![fitness bunner](https://github.com/Dorice97/Health-Fitness-Analysis/blob/master/fitness_bunner%20.png)  

## Problem Statement
 Most adults aspire to loose weight and one question almost everyone asks themselves is; "What can i do to burn more calories?" For some, starting off the fitness journey becomes a challenge
 due to fear arising from demographic , health status and lifestyle differences.  
 This study is focused on exploring how demographic, health condition and other physical activity patterns contribute to number of calories burned.
 The goal is to uncover meaningful patterns that can inform personalized fitness recommandations and improve health outcomes.  

 ## Objectives
   1.  Identify effective physical activities that can be considered for calorie burn
   2.  To determine if other lifestyle behaviors and health conditions  affect number of calories being burned
   3.  Explore gender and age based differences in fitness behaviors.
      
## Scope
 The scope of this project is to analyze patterns, trends, and relationships in health and fitness data to understand the factors that influence calories burned among participants.
Using data collected from activity tracking devices, the analysis focuses on 3000 participants who took part in different physical activities,presented varying health coditions and
lifestyle behaviors from January to December 2024.  
To achieve the project objective project analysis criteria was considered.  
  -  Data cleanup
  -  univariate analysis
  -  bivariate analysis
  -  multivariate analysis
 
 ## 1. Identify effective physical activities that can be considered for calorie burn
 - From the study it was discovered that physical activities may burn different amounts of calories depending on the internsity.  
 - Activities like High intensity interval training (HIIT), running and cycling lead to higher amount of calorie burn.  
 - Walking and yoga led to less calorie burn, they are less intensive activies as well.
![calorie vs activity](https://github.com/Dorice97/Health-Fitness-Analysis/blob/master/calorie_vs_activity_bar_plot.png)

 - A comparison of calories burned vs bmi showed that overweight and obese individuals burn more calories compared to underweight and normal.
   
![calorie vs bmi](https://github.com/Dorice97/Health-Fitness-Analysis/blob/master/calorie_vs_bmi_box_plot.png)
 
 ## 2. To determine if other lifestyle behaviors and health conditions  affect number of calories being burned
 A detailed assessment was done on :
 - calories burned vs smocking status
 - Calories burned vs health condition
 - calories burned vs sleep hours.
Accross both analysis it was concluded that calories burned are relatively the same and not influenced by a participants health condition and smocking status.
More calories are burned with 6 to 8 hours of sleep.
![calorie vs sleep](https://github.com/Dorice97/Health-Fitness-Analysis/blob/master/calorie_vs_sleep_scatter_plot.png)

Irrigardless of someone being a smocker or a non smocker  the tend to bun similar amount of calories on similar activities
![calorie vs smocking vs activity](https://github.com/Dorice97/Health-Fitness-Analysis/blob/master/calorie_vs_smocking_vs_activity_box_plot.png)

## 3. Explore gender and age based differences in fitness behaviors
A focus on age groups vs activity types revealed that most participants engaging in physical activities were in age group 41-60.  
- Underlying reasons might be self awareness and general knowledge on importance of fitness to their body.
- HIIT, walking, weight training and yoga are the common activities among individual of age group 41-60.
- Individuals above 61 are least involved in physical activities.  
![calorie vs activity age](https://github.com/Dorice97/Health-Fitness-Analysis/blob/master/calorie_vs_activity_age_bar_plot.png)


A plot on Calories burned vs age group by gender displayed:  
- Male indviduals burn more calories compared to females and other gender.  
- This might be due to males engaging in more intense execises compared to females or training longer compared to females.  
![calorie vs age, gender](https://github.com/Dorice97/Health-Fitness-Analysis/blob/master/calorie_vs_age_gender_bar_plot.png)

## Conclusion
From the study it is was concluded that;
1. All physical activities contribute to calorie burn however engaging in activities like HIIT, running and cycling can burn relatively high amount of calories compared to less intensive activities like yoga.
2. People with high body mass index(overweight and obese) can burn more calories even with less intensive exercises
3. Lifestyle behaviors like smocking and health do not determine amount of calories burned, whether asmatic or a smocker once tends to burn same amount of calories when during exercise.
4. Age and gender does not determine amount of calories burned however , activity type and body mass index does.
